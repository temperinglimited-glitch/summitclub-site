Privacy notice

Who is responsible

Tempering NI Limited, trading as Summit Club, is responsible for the personal information used to run direct bookings. Contact bookings@summitclub.co or write to 10C Clontafleece Road, Burren, BT34 3QS. This notice covers the website, bookings, guest registration and related correspondence. Please share it with other members of your party whose details you provide.

What we collect and why

We use the lead guest’s name, email, phone and postal address, stay dates, party numbers, children’s ages, bed choices, extras, payment references and booking correspondence to arrange the stay and deal with changes or problems. We rely on the booking contract for the lead guest and legitimate interests in administering the stay for other party members. We use necessary visitor-register details to meet legal obligations. We use security logs and records needed to prevent fraud, protect the service or resolve disputes on the basis of legitimate interests.

Visitor register

We collect names, home addresses, nationalities and stay dates for guests aged 16 or over. Where travel-document rules apply, we also ask for the document number, place of issue and next destination, including its address if known. We do not request passport scans. Lead guest contact details and party information are required to make a booking; visitor-register information is required before or on arrival. Children’s full names and dates of birth are not requested in the booking form.

Payments and service providers

Stripe handles card details on its payment page; our booking database does not store full card numbers or CVCs. Cloudflare hosts and secures the website and booking records. Resend sends booking emails. Our authorised team uses email, including Google Gmail, to handle enquiries and booking updates. Website fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which receives connection information such as IP address. We share necessary information with advisers or authorities where required, and limit staff access to what their work needs.

International processing

Some providers process information outside the UK. Before live bookings, we must confirm the provider contracts and transfer arrangements. Where a restricted transfer is made we use an applicable UK adequacy arrangement or appropriate contractual safeguards and the required assessment. Contact us for details of the arrangements applicable to your information. This notice does not promise that all information stays in Ireland or the UK.

How long we keep it

Visitor-register details are retained for at least 12 months after the stay; we review them after that and delete additional registration and travel-document details when no longer required, unless a legal or dispute-related reason requires longer retention. Necessary accounting and transaction records are kept for six years from the end of the company financial year concerned, or longer where law requires. Other correspondence and security records are kept only as long as needed for their purpose or a specific dispute. We review retention and restrict access to archived records; deletion is not currently automatic.

Cookies, messages and choices

The booking service uses necessary security and sign-in storage, and session storage for secure booking and registration access in your browser. Stripe may use necessary cookies and storage on its checkout. We do not currently add advertising or analytics cookies. Optional tracking would require a separate choice before activation. Booking confirmations and practical stay messages are service communications. Booking does not subscribe you to marketing; any marketing choice must be separate and optional. Do not send health information or other sensitive details through the free-text booking notes. Contact us to discuss a suitable way to handle an access or allergy enquiry.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask to access or correct information, erase it, restrict use, receive portable information or object to processing based on legitimate interests. In particular, you can object to our legitimate-interest use and to any direct marketing. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it. These rights have legal limits, including necessary legal record keeping. Contact bookings@summitclub.co. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/. We do not use solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about guests.
