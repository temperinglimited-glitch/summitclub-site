Your booking and payment

Check your chosen lodge, dates, guests, bed setup, extras and full GBP price before continuing to Stripe. The total includes applicable taxes and the charges shown in your quote; cleaning is included. Full payment is due on booking. A temporary checkout hold is not a confirmed booking. The contract is formed when payment is successfully verified and we confirm the booking. If a payment needs manual review, we will contact you before confirming the stay. Test bookings are clearly labelled and take no real payment.

Guests and the lodge

The lead guest must be at least 18, stay at the lodge and provide accurate contact and party details. Adults are 18 or over; children are under 18 and their ages are recorded as at arrival. Each full lodge has two bedrooms, each with an en-suite shower room. Maximum occupancy is four guests, except two adults and three children may stay when the children’s sofa bed is reserved. Four adults cannot be combined with children. The sofa bed is for children only. Do not exceed the confirmed occupancy; contact us to agree any change.

Arrival and your stay

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is by 11am. We will send practical arrival information separately; contact us if it has not arrived before you travel. Complete the visitor register before or on arrival and check the entries with us on arrival. Please look after the lodge, supervise children and respect neighbouring guests. Ordinary pets are not accepted; assistance dogs are welcome. Contact us before booking if you need information about access or adjustments so we can discuss what is suitable.

Breakfast hampers and requests

Hampers are optional paid extras. Each hamper supplies ingredients for one generous breakfast for the advertised party size, ready for you to cook in the lodge. We stock it before arrival and put chilled items in the fridge. The charge is per hamper, not per person or per night. Tea, coffee, cooking oil, salt and pepper are standard lodge essentials. Contents and allergen information must be available before purchase and with delivery. Contact us before buying if you have a food allergy or dietary question; do not put medical information in the booking notes. Special requests are not guaranteed unless we agree them in writing.

Cancellation by you

Full payment on booking. Cancel 30 or more days before arrival: full refund; 7–29 days: 50% refund; 0–6 days or no-show: no scheduled refund. Any amount retained is limited to reasonable loss, with savings and re-letting taken into account. We count calendar days between receiving your cancellation and the arrival date, using Northern Ireland local dates. Send cancellation requests to bookings@summitclub.co and quote your reference; we will acknowledge them. We take reasonable steps to re-let and review the refund if we reduce our loss. There is no separate 14-day change-of-mind cancellation right for accommodation booked for specified dates. This does not affect rights where we fail to provide the contracted service. We recommend suitable travel insurance.

Changes and refunds

Ask us about changes before making a replacement booking. Changes depend on availability and any agreed price difference; they take effect only when confirmed in writing. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; bank processing times vary. We will confirm the amount and when the refund has been submitted.

Damage

We do not take a fixed damage deposit. Tell us promptly about damage or breakages. We may seek reasonable, evidenced costs for damage beyond normal wear and tear caused by your party. We will explain the evidence and amount, take account of age and condition, and discuss it with you. We will not automatically charge a saved card for damage.

If something goes wrong

Report a problem as soon as possible so we have an opportunity to put it right. If we cannot provide your booked accommodation, we will discuss alternatives with you and refund any unprovided service if you do not accept an alternative. Your statutory rights and any other legal remedies remain. We do not exclude liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or anything the law does not allow us to exclude. Northern Ireland law applies, without removing mandatory consumer protections or court rights available where you live. Send complaints to bookings@summitclub.co.
