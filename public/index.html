Roscaire and Finn

Finn, the giant of summer, hurled a boulder from Slieve Foy at Roscaire, the giant of winter, and buried him on Slieve Martin. The stone is still there. Our lodges carry their names: Lodge 1 is Roscaire, and Lodge 2 is Finn.

The Cloughmore, or Big Stone, above Rostrevor. Legend from Slieve Foy, Wikipedia.

Before you book

How many people can stay?

Up to four adults per cabin; children cannot be added to a four-adult booking. Families of two adults and three children can stay using the sofa bed. The sofa bed is for children only and must be reserved in advance for £30 per booking, plus any additional guest charges shown in your quote. Book both cabins for bigger groups.

Can we book just one bedroom?

On selected low-season dates, up to two guests can book the lodge with one bedroom. You still have the kitchen, living area and bathroom to yourselves; the second bedroom remains closed.

Check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 11am.

Can we bring the dog?

No dogs, so the linen and the Mourne Textiles throws stay fresh for every guest, including those with allergies. Assistance dogs are always welcome.

Is there a cleaning fee?

No. Cleaning is included in the price.

What if we need to cancel?

Cancel 30 or more days before arrival: full refund. At 7–29 days: 50% refund. At 0–6 days or for a no-show: no scheduled refund. Any amount we retain is limited to reasonable loss, allowing for savings and re-letting. We recommend travel insurance.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights.

Is it open all year?

Yes. Winter is quieter on the hills.
